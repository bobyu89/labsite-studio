About

關於我們

Good care starts with good questions. We begin at the bedside and work with patients, families and nurses to find the questions worth studying.

Founded in 2018 (sample), the lab combines quantitative and qualitative methods to study chronic illness care, nursing education and digital health interventions.

(Sample) Long-standing partnerships with hospital nursing departments and community health centres bring our findings back to wards and neighbourhoods as tools nurses can use.
