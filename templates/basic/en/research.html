Research

研究方向

We study what patients and carers need in real clinical settings, and turn it into evidence for better care.

Research Areas

研究方向

  • Chronic illness self-management

    We develop and test nurse-led education and follow-up models that help people with diabetes and heart failure stay well at home.

    • Type 2 diabetes
    • Heart failure
    • Patient education

  • Nursing education and simulation

    Scenario-based simulation and feedback design that narrow the gap between classroom and ward for students and new graduate nurses.

    • Simulation
    • Clinical reasoning
    • New graduates

  • Digital health and telecare

    Digital tools that patients and families actually want to use, tested for safety and effect.

    • Telecare
    • Mobile health
    • User research
